## 📌 Mission & Overview

**Discord Moderation Academy** is dedicated to bridging the gap between informal chat moderation and professional community operations. We provide server administrators, safety teams, and community moderators with the technical infrastructure, structured documentation, and behavioral standards required to build scalable, resilient online spaces.

* **Standardized Playbooks:** Universal protocols for conflict resolution, incident escalation, and appeal handling.
* **Evidence-Based Benchmarks:** Practical evaluations assessing context analysis, policy enforcement, and proactive threat management.
* **Intelligent Tooling:** Purpose-built Discord automation and AI-augmented retrieval systems designed for high-volume moderation workflows.

## 🎯 What We Do

We are not an informal chat group; we operate as a dedicated **certification ecosystem and talent network**:

* **Practical, Rigorous Examinations:** Standardized evaluations built and validated in collaboration with leading bot developers and senior community architects.
* **Verified Talent Pipeline:** Direct matchmaking connecting certified, credentialed moderators with verified server owners and enterprise networks.
* **Deterministic Frameworks:** Universal playbooks covering escalation ladders, raid mitigation, evidence preservation, and fair appeal standards.

## 📋 Examination Framework & Tested Domains

The Moderation Academy evaluation assesses operational instincts, policy comprehension, and technical mastery rather than passive memorization. Candidates undergo a timed **26-question multi-format evaluation** across five core disciplines:

| Domain | Scope & Competencies Evaluated |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Platform Governance** | Discord Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and modern Policy & Safety enforcement updates. |
| **Incident De-escalation** | Conflict mediation, defusing bad-faith actors, and managing active crisis scenarios without escalating friction. |
| **Proportional Enforcement** | Contextual punishment ladders (warn, timeout, kick, ban) balanced against severity, offender history, and user intent. |
| **Technical Infrastructure** | Advanced AutoMod regex filters, permission overrides, channel hierarchy, and role privilege containment. |
| **Evidence Handling & Logging** | Snowflake ID preservation (User, Channel, Message), contextual capture, and evidentiary chain-of-custody for Trust & Safety reporting. |

#### 🎯 Requirements
* Active **`@D-4`** or **`@D-3`** credential held for at least **1 month**.
* **100% score** completed across all preparatory scenario modules.
* **1–2 hours** operational availability with clear voice audio and screen-share capability.

#### 🔄 Session Overview & Policies
| Attribute | Rule / Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 30 minutes minimum, up to 2 hours maximum (concluded at examiner's discretion). |
| **Grading Window** | Up to **1 calendar month** (full panel validation across telemetry and grading rubrics). |
| **Retake Frequency** | Once passed, credentials do not expire. Only targeted refresher modules apply for major Discord updates. |
| **Failure Policy** | Re-evaluation permitted after a **3-month** cooling period. |

</details>

### 🔬 Assessment Architecture & Testing Mechanics

The certification session utilizes three structured evaluation models:

* **Objective Analysis (Single / Multiple Choice):** Validates technical Discord mechanics, rule boundaries, and explicit policy thresholds.
* **Scenario Case Studies (Open Written Response):** Simulated server incidents requiring explicit justification of immediate interventions, escalation rationales, and post-incident auditing.
* **Triage & Priority Identification:** High-velocity scenarios featuring simultaneous violations where candidates must establish an accurate intervention queue.
